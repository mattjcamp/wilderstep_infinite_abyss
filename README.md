## Features

- Tactical D&D inspired turn-based combat system
- Open world design
- Built for expansion packs
- Procedurally generated dungeons and intentionally crafted adventures
- Classic chess-like battle arena with lighting effects and obstacles
- 8 Character classes and 5 races with unique ablities to suit your playstyle
- Rich sorcerer and priest spells
- Quests and random encounters
- Monsters with unique abilities
