## The Lost Gods
- There was a time, after the Elder Gods arose and before the current age, where gods roamed Varnfell. Each god had his or her own domain, but there was a great war. Knowledge of the gods passed from the world of Men. The common folk do not know exactly why that happened, but there are rumors of a great cosmic war that ended in the loss of the gods. Rumors linger that the these gods went into hiding and are still present in Varnfell. Some people believe that this knowledge is hidden in the traditions of secret cults, wizard guilds and in the halls of power. Occasionally, movements arise intersted in finding the gods and restoring the old ways. But, these are never successful and always end in tragedy.

## The Deep Ones
- When the gods departed, Varnfell became a wild and dangerous place. No longer could men, elves, dwarves or halflings petition local spirits to bless a harvest or protect an army. Twisted versions of men started to appear. The dead rose and feasted on the living. Straying from the paths of men was risky. Kingdoms were unable to defend their people or trade. Varnfell became a shell of what it once was.
- Many people believe that The Deep Ones are the last of the gods and they petitioned the Deep Ones to come and rescue the races of Varnfell that were left behind. Back in these long lost times, The Deep Ones emerged from their watery kingdom and filled the void left from the other gods. But, they exacted a heavy price. Villages who prayed to The Deep Ones were required to sacrifice their most precious citizens, to agree to perform acts of villany and the priests of The Deep Ones were expected to live a life of corruption and deceipt. 
- Anyone who questioned whether The Deep Ones were truly gods or something else entirely disappeared, found their families ruined or their material circumstances made dire.

### Adding New Players

Invite specific people (best for now / a trusted test group). You add their email addresses to the Access policy. Steps:

- Go to one.dash.cloudflare.com > Zero Trust → Access → Applications.
- Open your publish-API application (the one protecting …workers.dev/login).
- Open its Policies → edit the existing "Allow" policy.
- In the Include rule, add the new players' emails — either as individual Emails, or switch it to Emails ending in a domain (e.g. everyone @yourschool.edu) if you want to admit a whole group at once.
- Save
