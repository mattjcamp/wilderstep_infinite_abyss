## The Lost Gods
- There was a time, after the Elder Gods arose and before the current age, where gods roamed Varnfell. Each god had his or her own domain, but there was a great war. Knowledge of the gods passed from the world of Men. The common folk do not know exactly why that happened, but there are rumors of a great cosmic war that ended in the loss of the gods. Rumors linger that the these gods went into hiding and are still present in Varnfell. Some people believe that this knowledge is hidden in the traditions of secret cults, wizard guilds and in the halls of power. Occasionally, movements arise intersted in finding the gods and restoring the old ways. But, these are never successful and always end in tragedy.
