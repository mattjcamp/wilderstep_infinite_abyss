## The Deep Ones
- When the gods departed, Varnfell became a wild and dangerous place. No longer could men, elves, dwarves or halflings petition local spirits to bless a harvest or protect an army. Twisted versions of men started to appear. The dead rose and feasted on the living. Straying from the paths of men was risky. Kingdoms were unable to defend their people or trade. Varnfell became a shell of what it once was.
- Many people believe that The Deep Ones are the last of the gods and they petitioned the Deep Ones to come and rescue the races of Varnfell that were left behind. Back in these long lost times, The Deep Ones emerged from their watery kingdom and filled the void left from the other gods. But, they exacted a heavy price. Villages who prayed to The Deep Ones were required to sacrifice their most precious citizens, to agree to perform acts of villany and the priests of The Deep Ones were expected to live a life of corruption and deceipt. 
- Anyone who questioned whether The Deep Ones were truly gods or something else entirely disappeared, found their families ruined or their material circumstances made dire.
